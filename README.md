# A Little World Made for You
### *Commercial Creator Edition — Interactive Digital Birthday Short Film*

> **“Someone made something for you.”**  
> Designed as an interactive 60–90 second cinematic experience for smartphones.

---

## ✦ Commercial Seller Workflow (Sell to Customers)

As a creator selling this experience, **your customers and their gift recipients must NOT be able to edit or tamper with the gift**. The experience is built with a **Protected Creator Studio**:

### 1. How You (The Seller) Access the Creator Studio
You have three secret ways to open the Creator Studio:
* **Option A (Instant URL parameter)**: Add `?admin=true` or `?edit=1` to the link:  
  `https://yourdomain.com/?admin=true`
* **Option B (Desktop Shortcut)**: Press the **`E`** key on your keyboard.
* **Option C (Secret Mobile Tap)**: In the final scene, tap the small footer line (*“Made with genuine care, just for you.”*) **3 times** and enter your PIN (Default: `1234`).

### 3. How to Deliver to Your Customer

1. Open the Creator Studio (`?admin=true` or PIN `1234`).
2. Choose the theme: **Golden Celebration** (candlelight birthday), **Midnight Slate** (male), **Rose Atelier** (female), or **Noir & Gold**.
3. Type the recipient's name (e.g. *“DANIEL”*, *“EMILY”*, *“LEUL”*), title, and personal message.
4. Manage Memory Photos:
   - **Click any thumbnail** to directly replace that exact photo.
   - **Use ▲ / ▼ buttons** to reorder any photo to position #1 (Top of the Deck).
   - **Click “Clear Demo Photos”** to remove the stock examples with 1 tap.
   - **Upload unlimited photos** via multi-file upload or direct image URLs (photos automatically scale to lean 720px format for silky 60fps performance).
5. Choose delivery:
   - **Option 1**: Click **“Copy Customer Link”** for a clean, tamper-proof share link (`#g=...`).
   - **Option 2**: Click **“Export HTML File”** to download a single self-contained `.html` file with the customer's custom gift baked in.

## ✦ The 8-Scene Cinematic Journey

1. **Scene 1: Wake It Up** (`#scene-wake`) — Total dark silence. A single warm light point breathing in negative space. *“Someone made something for you.”* $\rightarrow$ *“Touch the light.”* Touching it triggers haptics and the ambient drone.
2. **Scene 2: The World Wakes Up** (`#scene-wish`) — Atmospheric 35mm projector dust motes drift across the screen, physically deflecting when the user drags their finger. Paced kinetic typography: *“Before the candles… make one wish. Not for anyone else. For you.”*
3. **Scene 3: The Glowing Light Orb** (`#scene-orb`) — A tactile celestial light orb that deforms elastically when touched, emitting warm embers and crystalline tones.
4. **Scene 4: Floating Physical Photographs** (`#scene-memories`) — 3D photographic prints floating loosely on a dark plane with realistic paper borders, analog drop shadows, and true touch physics (drag, tilt, velocity flick/swipe to dismiss, or spring-back).
5. **Scene 5: The Flash Montage** (`#scene-montage`) — Cinematic hush: *“Look at everything you've already lived.”* Followed by a rhythmic analog camera shutter montage with optical flash blooms.
6. **Scene 6: The Choice** (`#scene-choice`) — *“For the year ahead… What should there be more of?”* Four floating words: `ADVENTURE`, `PEACE`, `PEOPLE`, `STORIES`. Selecting one centers and enlarges it while the others disperse, revealing a tailored philosophical response.
7. **Scene 7: Final Build-up** (`#scene-buildup`) — The chosen word condenses into a single light point. *“Whatever happens… make it yours.”*
8. **Scene 8: The Reveal Climax** (`#scene-reveal`) — Monumental typography: **[RECIPIENT NAME]** $\cdot$ **Happy Birthday**, personal message, restrained golden stardust explosion, and a majestic orchestral chord swell synthesized in Web Audio.

## ✦ Technical Highlights

- **Zero External Dependencies**: Pure HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript.
- **Web Audio Generative Engine**: Ambient analog drone, paper slide friction sound, vintage mechanical camera clicks, and climactic orchestral chord swell synthesized directly in browser. Zero broken audio links.
- **Canvas Particle Physics**: 60fps projector dust, finger touch deflection, and multi-depth climax bloom.
- **Mobile-First (`390 × 844 px`)**: Safe-area insets, `100svh`, zero vertical scrolling. Desktop automatically presents the experience in a studio smartphone preview.
